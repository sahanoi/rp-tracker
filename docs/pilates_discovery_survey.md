# Reformer Pilates Discovery Survey

The purpose of this survey is to bridge the gap between "standard gym tracking" and the "art of Pilates." We want to build an app that actually understands how a Pilates body works.

## Section 1: The "Soul" of Pilates (Principles)
1. **The Big Six:** Pilates relies on Centering, Concentration, Control, Precision, Breath, and Flow. If we were to "gamify" or track these, which one is the most important for a practitioner to see after a session?
2. **The "Quality" Gap:** In the gym, 10 reps are 10 reps. In Pilates, 5 perfect reps are better than 15 sloppy ones. How do you (as a teacher) measure "quality of movement" without just counting?
3. **Mind-Body Connection:** Do you think it's valuable to track "Mental Focus" or "Breath Synchronicity" as a metric?

## Section 2: Mechanics & Equipment (The "Reformer" Factor)
4. **Springs vs. Weights:** Unlike gym weights, less spring tension often makes an exercise *harder* (due to stability). How should we record springs? (e.g., "Two Yellow, One Red" or a calculated "Tension Level"?)
5. **Apparatus Setup:** For a single exercise, does the "Footbar Position" or "Headrest Setting" change often enough that it needs to be tracked/remembered?
6. **Props:** How often do you add "Magic Circles," "Balls," or "Weights" to Reformer moves? Does the app need a "Plus Prop" toggle?

## Section 3: Tracking & Progress (The User Experience)
7. **The Transition "Flow":** In Pilates, moving from one exercise to the next is part of the work. Should the app track "Transition Time" or strictly the exercises?
8. **Progression:** How do you know someone is "leveling up" in Pilates? Is it doing the same move with *less* support, or *more* repetitions?
9. **Hevy Comparison:** As a user of gym apps, what is the #1 thing you find "annoying" or "incorrect" when trying to log a Pilates session in a standard gym app (like Hevy)?

## Section 4: The Teacher's Perspective
10. **Session Goals:** When planning a session, do you think in terms of "Muscle Groups" (Gym style) or "Spine Planes" (Flexion, Extension, Lateral, Torsion)?
11. **Client Feedback:** What is the most common piece of data your clients ask you about their progress?

---
*Note to User: These questions are designed to uncover whether we need a literal clone of Hevy's UI (Reps/Weight) or a more "Fluid" UI that prioritizes Settings and Quality.*
